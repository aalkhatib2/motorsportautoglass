SMS Consent Record

Effective date: July 23, 2026

This page documents the consent on file for the Motorsport Autoglass automated lead-notification SMS campaign (A2P 10DLC, use case: Account Notification).

Scope of this campaign

  • Messages are sent to a single, pre-consented recipient: the owner of Motorsport Autoglass LLC.
  • No customers or members of the public can opt in to this campaign.
  • No SMS opt-in is collected on any public web page. The public booking form on the business website collects customer service requests only — it does not enroll any visitor in SMS messaging.
  • Messages consist solely of internal operational alerts notifying the business owner when a customer submits a booking request through the business website.

Example of a message sent

Every message in this campaign uses the same fixed format — an automated internal lead alert delivered to the owner when a customer submits a booking request through the website. Only the customer's details change. Below is a representative example (placeholder data), identical to the sample messages registered with the campaign:

Motorsport Autoglass: New booking

Name:    Jane Doe
Phone:   (813) 555-1234
Email:   jane.doe@example.com
Vehicle: 2022 Toyota Camry (Sedan)
Service: Windshield Replacement
Cover:   GEICO
When:    Mon, Jul 21 - 8-11 AM
Where:   123 Main St, Tampa

Reply STOP to unsubscribe

Consent statement agreed to by the recipient

"I agree to receive automated text message notifications from the Motorsport Autoglass lead-notification system at the mobile number I provided, sent when a customer submits a booking request through my business website. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Recipient: Owner, Motorsport Autoglass LLC

Consent method: Direct written/verbal agreement, provided to the system administrator prior to campaign configuration

Consent obtained: July 2026, before any messages were configured or sent

Opt-out honored via: STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, OPTOUT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, REVOKE, END

Re-subscribe via: START or YES

Help via: HELP or INFO
